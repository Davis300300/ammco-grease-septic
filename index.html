What we pump

Liquid waste, start to finish

One crew, one truck, one record. We pump it, haul it, dispose of it at a permitted facility and hand you the paperwork.

Grease trap pumping & disposal

Full pump-out of the trap or interceptor, never a skim. Walls and baffles scraped, lids reseated, grease depth logged before and after.

Interior traps 20 to 100 gal · Interceptors 500 to 5,000 gal

Lift station pumping & cleaning

Wet well pump-down, rag and grit removal, wash-down, and a visual check of floats and pump intakes so your station runs quietly.

Municipal, HOA, hospital and campus stations

Commercial septic & holding tanks

Pumping for businesses off the sewer grid: travel centers, RV parks, churches, clinics, rural campuses and job-site holding tanks.

Tank located, lids exposed, effluent filter cleaned

Hydro-jetting & line cleaning

High-pressure jetting of kitchen drain lines, laterals and mains to cut grease, roots and scale back to bare pipe.

Up to 4,000 psi · 2" to 12" lines

Camera inspection & locating

Push camera with sonde locating to find the break, belly or blockage before anyone digs. You get the video and the footage marks.

Report delivered same day

Sewage pumping & disposal

Backups, overflows, flooded basements and holding-tank haul-off. Vacuumed, contained and manifested to a permitted facility.

Vac trucks 3,000 to 5,000 gal

Dewatering & sump pump-outs

Construction pits, utility vaults, elevator sumps and storm-flooded structures. Pumped down so your crews can get back to work.

Water, mud and sludge handled separately

Recurring route service

Monthly, quarterly or semiannual pump-outs set to your ordinance and your volume, confirmed the day before, with a record after every visit.

One contract, every location, same spec

Compliance

Inspection-ready, every visit

When the health inspector asks for your pumping records, the answer is a folder, not a scramble.

  • Signed manifest for every load

    Generator, transporter and receiving facility on one document, kept on file for the retention period your program requires.

  • Before and after photos

    Time-stamped shots of the trap on arrival and after the pump-out, so condition is never a matter of opinion.

  • Next-due date set on the record

    Interval based on the measured grease layer and your local ordinance. You will hear from us before it comes up.

  • Registered liquid waste transporter

    Permitted to haul grease, septage and sewage in Texas and Louisiana. Registration numbers available on request for your vendor file.

Why operators switch

Three things you stop worrying about

The schedule

Routes are planned around measured grease, not a calendar reminder you have to remember. If a trap is filling faster than expected, the interval tightens before it overflows.

The paperwork

Manifests, photos and due dates live in one record per unit. When a lease, a sale or an inspection needs history, it is a PDF away.

The disposal

Every load is hauled to a permitted facility under a signed manifest. Nothing is discharged on site, and the manifest copy lands in your record.
